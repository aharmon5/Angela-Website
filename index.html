About

A pioneering force in media and social justice, Angela Harmon built her foundation in television production, working with Netflix, NBC, and Amazon before earning an Emmy nomination for "Cash Cab" in 2013 and subsequent recognition and a nomination from the Academy Awards and NAACP Image Awards.

As founder of Angela's Harmony Productions and co-founder of Chromatic Black, she cultivated a network of 10,000 artist-activists, and co-raised over $10 million while launching impactful initiatives like "Keep Black Love Alive" during the COVID-19 pandemic. Her leadership has channeled $250,000 to emerging filmmakers and reshaped storytelling through the Constellation Incubator's Design Thinking program.

Selected for Harvard Kennedy School's inaugural Power, Innovation, and Leadership program, she continues to drive industry change through key positions at the Writers Guild of America East Women of Color Caucus, Level Forward, and Morehouse College Human Rights Film Festival Advisory Board. With her education and extensive production expertise, she exemplifies how creative vision paired with strategic leadership can transform both media and society.
